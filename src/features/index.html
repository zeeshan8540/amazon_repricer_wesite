Advanced Features Built for Amazon Sellers

Win the Buy Box faster, maintain strict profit margin safeguards, and scale your Amazon catalog seamlessly with SP-API automation.

⚡

Real-Time SP-API Push Sync

Unlike legacy scheduled repricers that check prices once an hour, our engine utilizes Amazon's official Selling Partner API (SP-API) Push Notifications. Price changes by competitors trigger sub-5-second adjustments to maintain continuous Buy Box possession.

🛡️

Profit Margin Floor Protection

Never sell at a loss. Define custom cost-plus formulas, minimum ROI limits, or fixed minimum price floors per SKU. If competitor price wars sink below your minimum margin, your repricer stays at your profit floor.

🎯

FBA vs. FBM Targeted Strategies

Differentiate your repricing strategy based on competitor fulfillment methods. Prime/FBA sellers can maintain higher prices against Merchant Fulfilled (FBM) sellers while staying competitive for the Buy Box.

📈

Algorithmic Buy Box Optimization

When your offer secures the Buy Box, the repricer dynamically raises your price in small increments to find the maximum price threshold where you retain the Buy Box, maximizing profit margin per sale.

🌐

Multi-Marketplace Support

Reprice across North American and European Amazon Marketplaces (US, Canada, UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain) from a unified dashboard with localized currency rules.

📊

Complete Audit & Execution Logs

Track every price movement with full historical transparency. Inspect why a specific price was adjusted, which competitor triggered the change, and the exact timestamp of execution.
