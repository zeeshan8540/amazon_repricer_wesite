Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

1. 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We want you to be completely satisfied with Amazon Repricer. All new subscription purchases (Starter, Business, Professional) come with a 7-day money-back guarantee. If you decide Amazon Repricer is not the right fit for your business within 7 calendar days of your initial payment, we will issue a full 100% refund of your subscription fee—no questions asked.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund within your 7-day guarantee period, please send an email to support@amazonrepricer.com with the following details:

  • Your account email address
  • Paddle Transaction ID or receipt number (found in your email receipt)
  • A brief note requesting a refund

Refund requests are processed within 1–2 business days. Approved refunds are credited back to your original payment method via Paddle.

3. Subscription Cancellation Policy

You can cancel your subscription renewal at any time. When you cancel:

  • Your subscription will remain active until the end of your current paid billing period.
  • You will not be charged for subsequent billing cycles.
  • To cancel, click the "Manage Subscription" link in your email receipt, or send a cancellation request to support@amazonrepricer.com at least 24 hours prior to your renewal date.

4. Merchant of Record Processing

All subscription payments, refunds, and cancellations are authorized and processed by Paddle.com Market Ltd, our official Merchant of Record. Paddle ensures secure payment handling, local tax compliance, and consumer protection.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding refunds or cancellations, please contact us at support@amazonrepricer.com.
