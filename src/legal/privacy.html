Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

Amazon Repricer ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal and business data. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and safeguard information when you use our website and repricing software.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Contact Data: Email address, business name, and account preferences when registering or contacting support.
  • Amazon Selling Partner Data: Product SKUs, ASINs, minimum/maximum price limits, and price history retrieved via authorized SP-API endpoints.
  • Payment & Billing Data: Payment credentials and billing address are processed directly and securely by our Merchant of Record, Paddle.com. We do not store full credit card numbers or sensitive payment details on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process collected data exclusively to:

  • Execute automated price updates to your Amazon catalog per your configured rules.
  • Manage active subscriptions and authenticate account access.
  • Provide customer support and technical troubleshooting.
  • Ensure compliance with Amazon Selling Partner API Developer Policies.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your account or catalog data with third parties for marketing purposes. We share data only with trusted service providers necessary for delivering the Service:

  • Paddle.com: Payment processing, tax compliance, and subscription management.
  • Amazon SP-API: Automated price update submission.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You may request the deletion of your account and associated Amazon listing data at any time by contacting support@amazonrepricer.com. Upon account cancellation, all stored SP-API credentials and listing tokens are permanently removed.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data rights, please contact support@amazonrepricer.com.
