Frequently Asked Questions

How does subscription billing work?
Subscriptions are billed monthly in advance via Paddle, our merchant of record. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your account settings or receipt link.
What is your refund policy?
We offer a full 7-day money-back guarantee for initial subscription purchases. If you are unsatisfied within 7 days, contact support@amazonrepricer.com for an immediate full refund. See our full Refund & Cancellation Policy.
Is my Amazon Seller Central data secure?
Yes. We connect via official Amazon Selling Partner API (SP-API) OAuth authorization. We never ask for or store your Amazon Seller Central login credentials or password.
Can I prevent repricing below my cost?
Absolutely. Every SKU has mandatory minimum price floor settings. Our repricer strictly enforces your profit floors and will never submit a price update below your established minimum.
